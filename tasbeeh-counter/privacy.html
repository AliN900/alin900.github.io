This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Tasbeeh Counter itself does not collect any personal information. All your Tasbeeh data, Dhikr preferences, and settings remain on your device and are never transmitted to any server we control.

However, our app displays advertisements through third-party ad networks (Google AdMob and Unity Ads) using ad mediation. These networks collect certain data as described in Section 3 below.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your Tasbeeh counting history
  • Your app preferences (language, theme, counter style, sound settings)
  • Your haptic feedback preferences

This data is stored using SharedPreferences and never leaves your device. It remains on your device until you delete the app or clear its data.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Data Collection

Our app uses ad mediation services to display advertisements from Google AdMob and Unity Ads. These partners may collect and process data to serve relevant ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud:

  • Device identifiers (including Android Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address and network info)
  • App interaction data (ad impressions, clicks)
  • Device information (model, operating system version, hardware specifications)

To learn more about how Google uses data when you use apps that partner with Google, please visit: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

For more information on Unity's data practices, please review Unity's Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings or via Google's Ads Settings.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for general audiences age 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

  • Internet: To load ads and app content
  • Network state: To check ad availability and network connection
  • Advertising ID: Required by Google AdMob and Unity Ads for ad delivery and analytics

We do NOT request access to your camera, contacts, photos, storage, or precise location.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Since we do not collect personal data on any server we control, there is nothing to access, modify, or delete on our end.

For data collected by Google AdMob and Unity Ads, you can exercise your rights and opt-out options directly through their respective privacy channels and your device's ad settings.

If you have contacted us via email and wish to have that correspondence deleted, email us at alialzwghaibi6@gmail.com. We will delete your email within 30 days. Email correspondence is retained for a maximum of 24 months.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app with an updated "Last updated" date.
