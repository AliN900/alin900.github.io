Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AHB.Dev ("we", "us", "our") handles your information when you use the mobile game Arrow Puzzle: Tap Maze Escape (the "App").

By using the App you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide voluntarily

  • Email address — only if you choose to create an account (optional).
  • Display name — optional, set during sign-up.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Anonymous user identifier — generated automatically on first launch, used to keep your progress in sync.
  • Gameplay data — current level, coins, trophies, high scores, settings preferences.
  • Device identifiers — collected by our advertising partner (Unity Ads) to serve relevant ads and prevent fraud.

1.3 What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name (unless you provide it), phone number, physical address, or precise location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To save your game progress and restore it across devices.
  • To display advertising that funds the App.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To detect and prevent cheating or abuse.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Appwrite (backend infrastructure) — appwrite.io/privacy
  • Unity Ads (advertising) — unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google Sign-In (optional login, if enabled) — policies.google.com/privacy

4. Advertising

The App displays ads. Unity Ads may collect device identifiers and use them to show relevant ads. You can limit personalized advertising in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

5. Data Retention

Your gameplay data is retained as long as your account exists. Anonymous data (unlinked to any email) is retained indefinitely, as it cannot be linked back to a specific person.

6. Your Rights

You can:

  • Play without creating an account (anonymous session).
  • Delete your account and all associated cloud data from Settings → Profile → Delete Account.
  • Request a copy of your data by emailing us.
  • Opt out of personalized ads via device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Cloud storage is handled by Appwrite with industry-standard encryption. We do not store payment information — the App is free with optional ads.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be noted in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
