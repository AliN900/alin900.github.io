How to Play

1

Tap to Launch

Tap any arrow to launch it off the board. Arrows slide in the direction they point—up, down, left, or right.

2

Turn Dots & Rerouting

Turn Dots redirect arrows mid-slide, opening new paths and creating surprising puzzle solutions.

3

Clear Obstacles

Rocks block arrows and require tactical bombs to clear. Sequence your taps carefully to clear every board.
